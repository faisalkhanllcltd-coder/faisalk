## Platform Overview

Quranific.com is a live, production online Quran academy platform owned, engineered, and marketed by Faisal Khan. The academy provides structured Quranic learning across multiple proficiency levels, combining high-speed edge delivery with transparent billing and modern UX.

The primary objective was to engineer an intuitive, high-converting learning platform that competes directly and feature-for-feature with established commercial LMS ecosystems like Tutor LMS Pro and LifterLMS, without the baggage and latency of legacy monolithic plugins.

## Technical Architecture

The frontend architecture and deployment pipeline were chosen for instant page loads, zero client-side hydration bloat on static content, and global edge caching:

- **Astro 6 & Svelte 5 (Runes)**: Leveraged Astro's server-first content rendering combined with fine-grained reactivity in Svelte 5 for interactive filtering and pricing widgets.
- **Tailwind CSS v4**: Streamlined atomic styling engine ensuring minimal CSS payload and responsive layouts across all device form factors.
- **Cloudflare Pages & Workers**: Global edge deployment providing near-zero Time to First Byte (TTFB) and secure serverless API handling for contact routing.
- **Resend**: Transactional email infrastructure delivering automated inquiry confirmations and student intake routing.

## Course Catalog & Funnel Redesign

The centerpiece of the platform release was a complete re-architecture of the academic catalog and conversion funnel:

- **9 Live Courses**: Structured academic offerings categorized by proficiency, from foundational phonetics to advanced Tajweed and memorization.
- **Category-Based Dynamic Filtering**: Interactive client-side filtering enabling prospective students to instantly isolate courses by target age group, track, and intensity.
- **Funnel-Aware Tiered Pricing**: Replaced an outdated single hero calculator with a comprehensive tiered comparison architecture, clearly delineating course deliverables, instructor ratios, and scheduling flexibility.

## Operational Model & Student Policies

To lower friction for prospective families and build trust upfront, the commercial engine implements transparent enrollment terms:

- **Flexible Invoicing Cadences**: Manual invoice billing across three distinct cadences: monthly, 6-month, and 12-month billing periods, with strictly zero lock-in beyond the current paid interval.
- **3-Day Free Trial**: Complete access for new student evaluations before any payment commitment.
- **Full-Month Satisfaction Guarantee**: Unconditional full-month satisfaction guarantee for enrolled students.
