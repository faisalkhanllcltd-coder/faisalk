## Executive Summary

CarePoint Health needed to modernize their public appointment scheduling system to comply with strict state accessibility mandates (WCAG 2.2 AA) while resolving a crippling 71% patient abandonment rate during initial provider booking.

I served as Frontend Architect & Accessibility Specialist, redesigning the user journey from discovery to appointment confirmation with rock-solid keyboard navigation, screen reader accessibility, and sub-50ms interaction latency.

## The Challenge

1. **Accessibility Non-Compliance**: The previous booking modal failed contrast checks, lacked ARIA landmarks, and was completely unusable via keyboard tab navigation.
2. **High Cognitive Load**: Complex multi-screen questionnaires overwhelmed patients seeking urgent care consultations.
3. **Sluggish Mobile Responsiveness**: Slow client hydration produced severe input delay (INP > 380ms) on low-end smartphones.

## Strategy & Technical Architecture

### 1. Semantic DOM & WCAG 2.2 AA Engineering
Rebuilt the entire appointment booking interface with semantic HTML5 elements (`<fieldset>`, `<legend>`, `<label>`), explicit focus traps for dialogs, and high-contrast color tokens tested rigorously across light and dark display modes.

### 2. Progressive Disclosure Intake Workflow
Redesigned the intake form using a progressive disclosure pattern that breaks questions into digestible, context-aware steps with live validation and auto-save capabilities.

### 3. Native Responsiveness & Low-Latency State
Avoided heavyweight client UI component libraries in favor of native browser elements and lightweight React 19 hooks, ensuring that every touch and keyboard interaction registered under 50ms INP.

## Measured Business Impact

- **100% WCAG 2.2 AA Audit Pass**: Certified compliant by independent third-party accessibility testers with zero critical, serious, or moderate violations.
- **+85% Increase in Completed Bookings**: Monthly online appointment volume grew from 640 to 1,184 within two months.
- **48ms Interaction to Next Paint (INP)**: Ultra-responsive interaction profile across all mobile devices.
- **-54% Patient Intake Abandonment**: Drop-off rates plummeted as friction points were eliminated.
