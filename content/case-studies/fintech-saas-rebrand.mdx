## Executive Summary

PayFlow Technologies provides corporate treasury automation and payment orchestration APIs. Despite high product maturity, their public marketing site was built on an unoptimized WordPress theme with sluggish page loads, zero structured data, and disjointed conversion tracking.

I led a full re-platforming to Next.js 16 and TypeScript, restructuring their organic acquisition architecture and engineering a conversion-optimized demo scheduling experience.

## The Challenge

1. **High Inbound Bounce**: Slow initial bundle load (LCP > 3.8s) turned away high-value enterprise financial officers browsing on mobile.
2. **Missing Long-Tail Search Presence**: High-intent search terms (such as ERP payment integrations and treasury compliance) had no dedicated pages or structured indexation.
3. **Broken Attribution Funnel**: Ad campaigns could not reliably attribute demo requests back to original search keywords and campaign parameters.

## Strategy & Technical Architecture

### 1. High-Performance Static Page Generation
Built marketing landing pages using Next.js static rendering. Page bundles were stripped of unnecessary runtime dependencies, keeping initial route JavaScript payloads under 65KB gzipped. Lighthouse mobile performance reached a flawless 100/100.

### 2. Programmatic Integration Directory
Architected an in-repo content pipeline generating over 45 integration landing pages (NetSuite, SAP, Stripe, QuickBooks) with consistent semantic hierarchy, schema.org `SoftwareApplication` data, and contextual demo triggers.

### 3. Friction-Free Enterprise Lead Capture
Engineered a dynamic multi-step qualification form with calendar booking integration, automated domain email validation, and client-side honeypot protection.

## Measured Business Impact

- **+215% Organic Search Traffic**: Over 18 top-tier keywords ranked on Page 1 within 60 days.
- **+182% Demo Request Rate**: Conversion rate from qualified visitors to scheduled demo meetings increased from 2.1% to 5.9%.
- **-37% Customer Acquisition Cost**: Improved organic inbound volume reduced reliance on expensive paid search CPC bidding.
- **100/100 Core Web Vitals**: Flawless performance across all major desktop and mobile audit profiles.
