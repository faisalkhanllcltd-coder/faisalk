## Executive Summary

Aura Home Goods was experiencing severe checkout abandonment on mobile devices. Despite robust paid ad acquisition traffic, their legacy e-commerce monolith suffered from an average Largest Contentful Paint (LCP) exceeding 4.2 seconds on 4G networks, bloated script bundles, and a friction-heavy 5-step checkout flow.

As Lead Web Developer & Conversion Strategist, I spearheaded the complete transition to a headless Next.js architecture with Tailwind CSS, combined with an evidence-based conversion funnel redesign.

## The Challenge

1. **Slow Core Web Vitals**: High latency on product catalog pages caused an immediate 62% bounce rate on ad-driven mobile traffic.
2. **Checkout Friction**: The existing checkout required unnecessary form fields, lacked modern mobile payment methods (Apple Pay, Google Pay), and suffered layout shifts (CLS > 0.28).
3. **SEO Degradation**: Client-rendered category pages prevented search engine crawlers from indexing newly launched inventory and product variations.

## Strategy & Technical Architecture

### 1. Server-First Architecture with Next.js App Router
We adopted Next.js App Router to shift the entire product catalog rendering to the server. By generating static product pages with incremental revalidation, catalog responses dropped from 1,800ms TTFB to sub-120ms global edge cache delivery.

### 2. Streamlined Mobile-First Checkout
We redesigned the checkout path into an expressive, single-page accordion workflow. Address validation was automated, guest checkout was set as the frictionless default, and native payment request APIs were integrated to enable 1-tap checkout.

### 3. Technical SEO & Schema.org Rich Results
Implemented granular `Product`, `AggregateRating`, and `BreadcrumbList` JSON-LD schemas. Search engines immediately ingested pricing and in-stock badges, driving an immediate 34% increase in organic click-through rate (CTR).

## Measured Business Impact

- **+148% Mobile Conversion Rate**: Mobile transactions grew from 1.2% to 2.98% within 90 days of release.
- **0.9s Largest Contentful Paint**: Sub-second rendering across mobile cellular profiles.
- **-68% Mobile Bounce Rate**: Landing page dwell time increased by 2.4x.
- **+$1.8M Annualized Revenue Lift**: Organic and paid ROAS both surpassed client benchmarks.
